1. Electric Current in Metals

  1. Explain the mechanism of electric conduction in metals and the term electrical resistance. Characterise resistivity and the dependence of resistance on the dimensions of the conductor and on temperature.
  2. State Ohm’s law for part of a circuit and for a complete circuit.
  3. Explain Kirchhoff’s first and second laws.

Task: In a branched circuit with two sources and three resistors, a current of I₁ = 100 mA flows through resistor R₁ and a current of I₂ = 150 mA through resistor R₂. The directions of the currents are shown in the diagram.

  1. Use Kirchhoff’s first law to determine the current I₃ through the middle resistor R₃.
  2. What would the current I₃ be if I₂ flowed in the opposite direction (out of junction A)?

2. Dynamics – Bullet in a Barrel

  1. State Newton’s laws of motion.
  2. Describe uniformly accelerated motion in a straight line (equations for velocity and distance).

Task: A bullet of mass m = 20 g starts from rest in the barrel of a rifle. In a time of t = 0.01 s it reaches a speed of v = 150 m/s. Assume constant acceleration and neglect resistive forces.

  1. Calculate the acceleration of the bullet.
  2. Determine the resultant force acting on the bullet.
  3. Calculate the length of the barrel travelled by the bullet while accelerating.
