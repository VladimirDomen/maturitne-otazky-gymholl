1. Electric Current

  1. Describe electric current as a physical phenomenon. What conditions are needed for it to exist? Define the quantity electric current.
  2. Give examples of voltage sources and explain the role of a source in an electric circuit.
  3. Explain the terms electromotive force (emf) and terminal voltage and the relation between them.

Task: A charge of Q = 30 C passes through a cross-section of a conductor in a time of t = 2 min.

  1. What is the current in the conductor?
  2. How many electrons pass through the cross-section of the conductor in this time?
  3. The current is drawn from a battery with an emf of 4.5 V and an internal resistance of 1 Ω. What is the terminal voltage of the battery and what is the resistance of the external part of the circuit?

2. Kinematics – v–t Graph

  1. Characterise uniform and uniformly accelerated (decelerated) motion in a straight line and give the equations for velocity and distance.
  2. How are acceleration and distance found from a graph of velocity against time?

Task: The graph shows the velocity of a body moving in a straight line as a function of time.

  1. Determine the type of motion in each section and calculate the acceleration in each of them.
  2. Determine the distances travelled in the individual sections and the total distance.
  3. Sketch the graph of distance against time s(t).
  4. Sketch the graph of acceleration against time a(t).
