1. Kinematics of a Particle

Theory

  1. Explain the terms mechanical motion, particle (point mass), frame of reference and the relativity of motion and rest.
  2. Define trajectory, distance travelled, velocity and acceleration.
  3. Classify motions by the shape of the trajectory and by the change of velocity. Give examples.

Calculation

A car travels along a straight road at 72 km/h. The driver starts braking uniformly with an acceleration of −1.8 m/s². Calculate the distance travelled by the car until it stops. Also determine the time needed to stop.
Part 2 of 3

2. Stationary Magnetic Field

Theory

  1. Describe a stationary magnetic field and the quantity magnetic flux density.
  2. Describe the magnetic field around a long straight current-carrying wire. Explain the right-hand grip rule.
  3. Explain the interaction between two parallel current-carrying wires.

Calculation

Two long parallel straight wires are 15 cm apart. The current in the first wire is 12 A and in the second 10 A; both currents flow in the same direction. Calculate the magnitude and determine the direction of the resultant magnetic flux density at the midpoint between the wires. Treat the surroundings as a vacuum.
Part 3 of 3

3. The Microworld

Theory

  1. Describe the structure of the atom and of the atomic nucleus.
  2. Explain radioactivity and briefly describe α, β and γ radiation.
  3. Explain the law of radioactive decay and the meaning of half-life.

Graphical task

Sketch a graph of the number of undecayed radioactive nuclei N against time t. Mark the initial number of nuclei and the half-life on the graph. Write the equation N(t) and explain the shape of the curve.
