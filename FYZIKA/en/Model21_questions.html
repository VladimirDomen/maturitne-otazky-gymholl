1. Alternating Current – Generation and Transformation

  1. Explain how an alternating voltage and current are generated in an alternator. What is a three-phase system?
  2. Explain the principle of a transformer. Why is electrical energy transmitted at high voltage?
  3. Describe different types of power stations and the energy conversions in them.

Task: An ideal transformer has N₁ = 5 000 turns on the primary coil and N₂ = 2 500 turns on the secondary coil. The primary voltage is U₁ = 230 V.

  1. Calculate the secondary voltage U₂.
  2. Determine the turns ratio and state how you define it.
  3. Decide whether it is a step-up or a step-down transformer.

2. Structure and Properties of Gases

  1. Describe the structure of gases from the point of view of molecular physics. What is an ideal gas?
  2. Write the equation of state of an ideal gas and characterise the basic processes in a gas.
  3. How is the work done by a gas found from a p–V diagram?

Task: An ideal gas of constant mass goes through the cycle A → B → C → D → A shown in the p–V diagram.

  1. Describe the individual processes between the states. For each, state whether the gas does positive, negative or zero work and how its temperature changes.
  2. Calculate the work done by the gas in each section and the total work W for the whole cycle.
