2. Electric Current

A charge of Q = 30 C passed through a resistor with resistance R = 15 Ω in a time of t = 2 min. Elementary charge e = 1.602 · 10⁻¹⁹ C.

  1. How many electrons passed through the cross-section of the conductor?
  2. Define electric current and calculate it.
  3. Define electric voltage. What was the voltage across the resistor?
  4. What would the voltage across a resistor with R = 50 Ω be at the same current?

3. Experiment – Stationary Magnetic Field

Use iron filings to show the magnetic field (filing patterns) of:

  1. a permanent bar magnet,
  2. a straight current-carrying conductor,
  3. a current-carrying loop (coil),
  4. two magnets placed with like poles and with unlike poles facing each other.

Draw the patterns, mark the direction of the field lines and explain the differences observed.
