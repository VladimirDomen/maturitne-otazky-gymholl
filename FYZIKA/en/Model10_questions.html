1. Structure and Properties of Gases

  1. Describe an ideal gas and explain its model.
  2. Define the root-mean-square speed of molecules and explain how it depends on temperature.
  3. State and explain the basic equation for the pressure of an ideal gas.
  4. Write the equation of state of an ideal gas and explain the individual quantities.
  5. Describe isothermal, isochoric and isobaric processes. Give the corresponding relations and the shape in a p–V diagram.
  6. Explain thermal processes with an ideal gas from the energy point of view using the first law of thermodynamics.

Task – pressure in a tyre: The air in a tyre at a temperature of 17 °C has an absolute pressure of 250 kPa. What will its pressure be when the temperature rises to 77 °C, if the volume of the tyre and the amount of air do not change?

2. Concave Spherical Mirror

An object of height y = 0.5 cm is placed in front of a concave spherical mirror with a radius of curvature R = 4 cm, at a distance a = 1 cm from the vertex of the mirror.

  1. Determine the focal length of the mirror.
  2. Construct the image of the object geometrically.
  3. Calculate the position and height of the image.
  4. Describe the properties of the image formed.

3. Experiment – Density of a Solid

Determine experimentally the density of a solid (for example a metal cylinder). Propose at least two methods of measurement – for a body of regular shape and for a body of irregular shape – and compare the results with the table value.
