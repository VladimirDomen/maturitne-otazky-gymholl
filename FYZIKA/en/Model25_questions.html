1. Atomic Nucleus

  2. Explain the terms mass defect and binding energy of a nucleus.

2. Electric Current in Electrolytes – Nickel Plating

  1. Explain electric conduction in electrolytes and state Faraday’s laws of electrolysis.

Task: A metal object with a surface area of S = 120 cm² is nickel-plated for t = 5 hours with a current of I = 0.3 A. The molar mass of nickel is M = 58.69 · 10⁻³ kg·mol⁻¹, nickel is divalent in the solution, the density of nickel is ρ = 8.8 · 10³ kg·m⁻³, F = 96 485 C·mol⁻¹.

  1. Calculate the mass of nickel deposited.
  2. What is the thickness of the nickel layer deposited on the object?
  3. What mass and thickness will the layer have if plating lasts t = 10 hours at a current of I = 0.3 A?
  4. What mass and thickness will the layer have at a current of I = 0.6 A for 5 hours? Compare the results of c) and d).

Use a vernier caliper to measure the dimensions of a small cylinder and determine its volume. Explain how the vernier scale is read.
