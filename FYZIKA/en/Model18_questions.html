1. Free Oscillations

  1. Describe oscillatory motion and the quantities that characterise it (period, frequency, angular frequency, phase).
  2. Explain the terms instantaneous displacement and amplitude of displacement.
  3. Write down and explain the kinematic equation of simple harmonic motion. What are free oscillations of an oscillator?

Task: The graph shows the displacement of a mass on a spring as a function of time.

  1. Determine the amplitude of displacement A.
  2. Determine the period T and the frequency f of the oscillation.
  3. Determine the initial phase φ₀ and write the equation of displacement y(t).
  4. Extension task: Calculate the velocity amplitude vmax and the acceleration amplitude amax.

2. Motion in a Gravitational Field – Horizontal Projection

  1. Describe the motion of bodies in the uniform gravitational field of the Earth (free fall, projectiles).
  2. Explain horizontal projection as a combined motion and give the equations for the coordinates and the velocity.

Task: A body is projected horizontally with a speed of v₀ = 60 m/s from a height of h = 60 m above level ground. Neglect air resistance; g = 10 m/s².

  1. Determine the position of the body at time t = 2 s.
  2. Determine the speed of the body at time t = 2 s.
  3. After what time does the body hit the ground?
  4. At what horizontal distance from the point of projection does it land?
