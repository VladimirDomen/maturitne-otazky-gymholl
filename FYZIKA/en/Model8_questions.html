2. Electromagnetic Waves

A progressive electromagnetic wave travels in the positive direction of the x-axis. The voltage varies harmonically with time. The voltage amplitude is A = 20 V, the frequency f = 10 MHz and the speed of propagation v = 300 000 km/s. At the initial point the initial phase is zero.

a) Write the equation of the progressive wave for the voltage u(x,t).

b) Calculate the instantaneous value of the voltage at a distance x = 3 m from the source at time t = 10−3 s.
