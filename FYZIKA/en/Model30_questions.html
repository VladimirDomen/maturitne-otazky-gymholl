2. Electric Current in Metals – Circuit

A source with voltage U = 12 V (neglect its internal resistance) is in series with a rheostat R₁ = 10 Ω and resistors R₂ = 20 Ω and R₃ = 30 Ω. Voltmeter V₁ is connected across R₁, voltmeter V₂ between points A and C. Treat the voltmeters as ideal.

  1. Find the total resistance of the circuit.
  2. Calculate the current in the circuit.
  3. Find the voltage shown by voltmeters V₁ and V₂.
  4. How do the voltmeter readings change if we increase R₁ to 30 Ω with the rheostat slider?

3. Experiment – Oscillation of an Air Column
