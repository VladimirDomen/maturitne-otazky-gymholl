1. Changes of State and Internal Energy

  1. Describe melting, solidification, evaporation, boiling, condensation, sublimation and deposition.
  2. Explain changes of state in terms of the kinetic theory of matter. Describe the changes in internal energy.
  3. Draw a phase diagram and explain the meaning of its regions, curves, the triple point and the critical point.

Calculation: Determine the change in internal energy when 3 kg of tin, initially at its melting point of 232 °C, melts completely. The specific latent heat of fusion of tin is 61 kJ/kg. Neglect the work associated with the change in volume.

2. Electron Shell – Electron in an Electric Field

Briefly describe the electron shell of an atom and the connection between energy levels and the emission and absorption of light.

An electron is accelerated from rest by an electric field through a voltage of 100 V. Neglect energy losses.

  1. Calculate the speed of the electron.
  2. Determine its de Broglie wavelength.

Constants: e = 1.602 · 10−19 C; me = 9.109 · 10−31 kg; h = 6.626 · 10−34 J·s.
