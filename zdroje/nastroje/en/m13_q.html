1. Electric Current in Semiconductors

  1. Explain intrinsic and extrinsic (doped) conduction in semiconductors. How does an N-type semiconductor differ from a P-type semiconductor?
  2. Describe how a PN junction and its depletion layer are formed.
  3. Explain the behaviour of a diode in forward and reverse bias and describe its current–voltage characteristic.
  4. Give practical applications of the PN junction in devices.

2. Mechanics of a Rigid Body – Beam on Two Supports

  1. Define the moment of a force about an axis of rotation and explain the term moment arm.
  2. State the principle of moments and the conditions of equilibrium of a rigid body.

Task: A horizontal beam of length l = 6 m is supported at its ends by two walls A and B. A load of mass m = 12 t rests on the beam. The beam presses on wall B with a force of 12 kN. Neglect the mass of the beam; g = 10 m/s².

3. Experiment – Inductance of a Coil and Capacitance of a Capacitor

Using an AC voltage source of known frequency, an ammeter and a voltmeter, determine the capacitance of a capacitor and the inductance of a coil. Propose the circuit, the measurement procedure and the evaluation. Explain why measuring with alternating current alone is not enough for the coil.
