  2. Describe the types of electromagnetic waves and compare their wavelengths, frequencies and energy.
  3. Analyse the speed of electromagnetic waves in a vacuum and in a medium.

Task: An electromagnetic wave with a wavelength of λ₀ = 240 nm in a vacuum passes into a medium in which it travels at v = 2 · 10⁸ m/s. Determine its frequency, its wavelength in this medium and the type of radiation.

2. Structure and Properties of Solids

  1. Explain the linear thermal expansion of solids.
  2. Describe tensile deformation: normal stress, strain and Hooke’s law.

Task: A steel rod has a length of l₀ = 1.5 m and a diameter of d = 20 mm. The coefficient of linear thermal expansion of steel is α = 1.2 · 10⁻⁵ K⁻¹ and Young’s modulus is E = 2.1 · 10¹¹ Pa.

  1. By how much does the rod extend when heated by 1 °C?
  2. What force is needed to extend the rod at constant temperature by the same amount as when it is heated by 1 °C?

3. Experiment – Outflow Speed of a Liquid

Using horizontal projection, determine the speed of a liquid flowing out of a small hole in the wall of a container. Propose the equipment, the measurement procedure and the calculation. Compare the result with the speed given by Torricelli’s formula.
