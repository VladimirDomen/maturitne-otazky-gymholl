1. Alternating Current

  1. Describe how an alternating voltage is produced. Express the instantaneous value of an alternating voltage and current as a function of time and draw their graphs.
  2. Explain the behaviour of a resistor, a coil and a capacitor in an AC circuit – their effect on the current amplitude and on the phase difference between current and voltage.

Task: A coil with negligible resistance is connected to an alternating voltage of 230 V with a frequency of 50 Hz. The current through it is 0.5 A. What is the inductance of the coil?

2. Molecular Physics and Thermodynamics – Calorimetric Equation

  1. Explain internal energy, heat and heat capacity. Describe the heat needed to warm a substance and the latent heat of fusion.
  2. Write the calorimetric equation and explain when it applies.

Task: A calorimeter with a heat capacity of C = 90 J/K contains 200 g of water at 80 °C. We put 150 g of ice into the water. The specific latent heat of fusion of ice is 334 kJ/kg, the specific heat capacity of ice is 2.1 kJ/(kg·K) and of water 4.18 kJ/(kg·K). Neglect heat losses to the surroundings.

  1. Write the calorimetric equation and find the final temperature of the system at equilibrium if the ice was at 0 °C.
  2. Find the final temperature of the system if the ice was at −15 °C.
