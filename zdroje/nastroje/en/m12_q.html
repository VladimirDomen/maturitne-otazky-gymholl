Calculation – steel cable

A steel cable consists of 15 identical steel wires, each with a diameter of 2.0 mm. The ultimate tensile strength of the steel is 1000 MPa.

Calculate the magnitude of the tensile force at which the cable would break. Assume the force is distributed evenly among the individual wires.

2. Photoelectric Effect

  1. Determine the threshold wavelength of the photoelectric effect.
  2. Determine the maximum kinetic energy of the emitted electrons.
  3. Calculate the maximum speed of the emitted electrons.
