1. Magnetic Field

  1. Describe the magnetic field of a permanent magnet. How do we represent a magnetic field and what are magnetic field lines?
  2. Describe the magnetic field of a straight current-carrying wire and of a coil. How do we determine the direction of the field lines?
  3. Describe the interaction of two parallel current-carrying wires when the currents are in the same direction and when they are in opposite directions.
  4. Describe the effect of a magnetic field on charged particles. Where is it used?

2. Fluid Mechanics – Flow in a Pipe

  1. Explain the terms ideal liquid, steady flow and streamline.
  2. Define volume flow rate and mass flow rate and state the continuity equation.
  3. State Bernoulli’s equation.

Task: Water flows through a horizontal pipe with a cross-section of S₁ = 20 cm² at a speed of v₁ = 8 m/s. The water pressure at this point is p₁ = 1.08 · 10⁵ Pa. Further on, the pipe widens to a cross-section of S₂ = 40 cm². Treat water as an ideal liquid, ρ = 1 000 kg/m³.

  1. Calculate the volume flow rate of the water.
  2. Calculate the mass flow rate of the water.
  3. At what speed does the water flow in the wider part?
  4. Extension task: What is the water pressure in the wider part?

3. Experiment – Specific Latent Heat of Fusion of Ice

Determine the specific latent heat of fusion of ice experimentally. Propose the apparatus, the measurement procedure and the evaluation. Set up the calorimetric equation and explain the terms internal energy, work and heat that you use in deriving it.
