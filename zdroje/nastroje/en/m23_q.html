2. Change of State – Heating of a Bullet

  1. Explain the terms internal energy, heat and specific heat capacity.
  2. Describe the changes of state of a substance. What are the melting point and the specific latent heat of fusion?

Task: A lead bullet at a temperature of t₀ = 40 °C hits a steel plate at a speed of v = 300 m/s and stops. Assume that all its kinetic energy is converted into the internal energy of the bullet. The specific heat capacity of lead is c = 125 J·kg⁻¹·K⁻¹, the melting point of lead is 327 °C and the specific latent heat of fusion of lead is lt = 23 kJ/kg.

  1. Explain the heating of the bullet from the point of view of energy.
  2. By how much would the bullet warm up if it did not melt?
  3. What is the minimum speed at which the bullet must hit the plate to warm up to the melting point of 327 °C?
  4. What happens to the energy if the speed of the bullet is greater than the calculated speed? What fraction of the bullet melts at a speed of 300 m/s?
