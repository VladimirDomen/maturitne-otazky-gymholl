2. Wave Optics – Interference in an Oil Film

There is an oil film of thickness d = 0.2 μm on the surface of water. Light from the air falls on it perpendicularly. The speed of light in a vacuum is c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s, in oil vₒ = 2 × 10⁸ m/s and in water approximately vw = 2.25 × 10⁸ m/s. Take the refractive index of air as 1.

  1. Explain how interference of the reflected light arises.
  2. Determine the refractive indices of oil and water and the phase changes on reflection.
  3. Determine the vacuum wavelengths at which the reflected light is reinforced and weakened.
  4. Select the results in the visible range of approximately 380–780 nm.

Draw the circuit diagram, record the values of voltage and current and plot the graph I = f(U). Explain the difference between the two directions.
